Keyboard Visualizer

EN / RU keyboard for demonstrations and screen recordings
Physical keyboard: Ctrl Shift Alt Win + key. Mouse mode: click a modifier to hold it, then click another key. Click the modifier again to release it.
Middle mouse button: click a key to keep it pressed (pulsing), click it again to release.
